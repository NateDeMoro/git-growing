## Contract fields used (conform to contract.ts on main; names below are the plan's, use the file's exact spelling)
- SceneFrame: time; layers (height ft, temperature, wind speed as the 2-minute average, wind direction); derived summary (inversion °F, speed shear, direction shear, Delta T, RH); sun elevation; cloud %; rain rate in/hr; rain probability; gusts; surface pressure and pressure history; look-ahead (sim only). Read 5 ft and 30 ft layers by height, not by array index.
- Product: volatility band, vapor pressure mPa, label wind maximum (default 10 mph), label minimum DropletClass.
- DropletClass: the eight ordered S572.3 classes from `droplets.ts`.
- Assessment: status, reasons (sorted worst first), recommended droplet class or none, the "no droplet size makes this safe" cause, mode.
- Reason: rule id, status, plain-language text with real numbers, source short name.
- Status: GO / CAUTION / STOP.
- Prefer the frame's derived summary values when present; recompute from layers only if the contract leaves them optional.

## Thresholds rule
Every cutoff (1.8°F, 3 mph, 2 mph margin, 10 mph gust excess, 5/10 mph, 45/90°, 2 mph calm floor, Delta T 2/10/12°C, 0.10 in/hr, 50% in 2 h, 2.0 hPa/h, 12 h, 3 h before sunset, 1 h after sunrise, 25% cloud, volatility bands 5/10 mPa) is read from `thresholds.ts`. No numeric literal for a cutoff in the engine. Each reason's source tag is the rule's source short name from that table, so the "Why these limits" panel and reasons can never disagree.

## Rule table (plan 5b)
Overall status is the worst rule status. Reasons sort STOP, CAUTION, GO.
- **Inversion** (30 ft minus 5 ft): 1.8°F or more STOP (Battany 2017 plus label "do not apply during inversions"); above 0 and under 1.8°F CAUTION (track brief). No droplet class clears it.
- **Wind window** (2-minute average, checked at both heights): under 3 mph CAUTION, STOP if an inversion is present (any positive inversion); over the product's label maximum STOP; within 2 mph of the maximum CAUTION (heuristic). Source: track brief plus Liberty ULTRA 2-minute sustained-wind definition.
- **Gusts**: above the label maximum CAUTION; more than 10 mph over it STOP. Heuristic.
- **Speed shear**: 5 mph or more CAUTION, 10 or more STOP. Heuristic numbers, track brief for flagging.
- **Direction shear**: 45° or more CAUTION, 90° or more STOP, only when both speeds are at least 2 mph. Use the shared angular-difference util (0 to 180). Heuristic.
- **Delta T at 5 ft** (GRDC 2025, wet bulb via Stull): under 2°C CAUTION; 2 to 10 GO; 10 to 12 CAUTION and requires Very Coarse or larger; over 12 STOP. Show Delta T in °C in the reason text (the guideline's unit).
- **Volatility** (PPDB/EFSA bands at 25°C: under 5 mPa low, 5 to 10 moderate, over 10 high): high band plus any positive inversion is STOP. Any band plus an inversion forecast within 12 h adds CAUTION "vapor can keep moving for hours after application" (EPA drift guidance). No temperature cutoff.
- **Inversion-timing hint** (Faber 2015): from 3 h before sunset until 1 h after sunrise, with cloud 25% or less, CAUTION "inversion likely forming or present".
- **Rain** (AMS Glossary): over 0 and up to 0.10 in/hr CAUTION; over 0.10 STOP. Rain probability over 50% in the next 2 h CAUTION "check the label's rainfast period" (heuristic).
- **Storm approaching** (NWS/FMH-1 PRESFR): pressure falling 2.0 hPa (0.06 inHg) per hour or faster CAUTION, computed from the frame's pressure history.
- **Droplet recommendation** (ANSI/ASABE S572.3): evaluate classes from the product's label minimum up to Ultra Coarse. Only two rules are class-dependent: the label minimum and the Delta T 10 to 12°C Very Coarse rule. Pick the finest GO class; else the finest CAUTION class with its reasons; else none, with "no droplet size makes this safe" naming the STOP cause. The headline status is the status at the recommended class, or STOP when none.

## Single-station mode
Same engine on 5 ft data only. Skip inversion, inversion-combined calm wind, high-volatility-any-inversion, speed shear and direction shear. Wind and gust rules use the 5 ft layer. The timing hint is the only inversion signal. Open question for the coordinator: whether the forecast-inversion vapor CAUTION also stays off (default: off, since it needs the tower's inversion). Beat A must come out GO here.

## Performance (runs per frame at 6 sim-hours/s, about 72 frames/s, times up to 8 classes)
- Pure: no I/O, no Date.now, no randomness, no mutation of inputs, no allocation-heavy work in the loop.
- Compute weather rules once per frame; apply only the two class-dependent rules per class, not eight full passes.
- Cache suncalc sunrise/sunset per calendar day (small module-level memo keyed by date is fine; still deterministic).
- Reason text built with simple templates; numbers rounded to one decimal.
